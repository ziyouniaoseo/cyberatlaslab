Buying Guide

How to Choose the Right VPN

Not all VPNs are created equal. Here's what to look for when choosing a VPN service.

Encryption & Protocol

Look for AES-256 encryption and modern protocols like WireGuard or OpenVPN. Avoid PPTP and older protocols.

No-Logs Policy

A verified no-logs policy means the provider doesn't store your browsing activity. Look for independent audits.

Server Network

More servers in more locations means better performance and more flexibility for geo-unblocking.

Device Compatibility

Ensure the VPN supports all your devices — Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and ideally router-level support.

Price & Value

Balance cost with features. Long-term plans offer the best value, but always check the refund policy first.
